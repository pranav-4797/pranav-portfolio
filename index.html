About Me

I thrive on turning complex problems into elegant, user-friendly solutions.

I'm an AI-focused full-stack developer and engineering student at MIT Academy of Engineering (MITAOE), Pune (2025–2029). My journey spans from creating AI-powered fraud detection systems to crafting real-time multiplayer games.
